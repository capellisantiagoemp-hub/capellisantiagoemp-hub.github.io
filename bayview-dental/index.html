Meet your dentist

Dr. Amalia Quiñones

Dr. Amalia Quiñones practices cosmetic and general dentistry at Bayview Dental in Fort Lauderdale. She was born in Cuba and has lived most of her life in South Florida. After working in the DC area for a few years, she moved back to be close to her family.

Her patients can always voice their concerns and trust they are treated with gentle care. Dr. Q, as many patients call her, is known for her compassion and integrity, and treats her patients with respect, honesty and, most notably, as family.

  • Doctorate in Dental Surgery, Howard University
  • Born in Cuba, rooted in South Florida

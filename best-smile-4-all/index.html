Your dentists

Meet the doctors behind the smiles.

Dr. Paola Andrea Piñeros, DDS

Owner dentist · English and Spanish
  • Dental degree from Universidad del Bosque in Bogotá, Colombia.
  • Completed the Advanced Education in General Dentistry program at the University of Florida.
  • Trained in oral surgery, restorative and implant dentistry, root canals, cosmetic dentistry and Invisalign.

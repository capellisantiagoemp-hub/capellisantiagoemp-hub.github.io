Our Story

A Family Tradition of Dental Care

Dr. Julio Antonio Llera established Llera Dental in 1984, right here on Davie Boulevard in Fort Lauderdale. Since then, he has built a practice rooted in trust, community, and genuine care for every patient.

Today, his son Dr. Antonio J. Llera works alongside him, bringing advanced training in cosmetic and implant dentistry. Together, they combine decades of experience with modern techniques — two generations dedicated to your smile.

Recognized by the community — acknowledged by El Heraldo de Broward and Hispanic Unity of Florida for pro bono services.

Hablamos Español — We proudly serve the Hispanic community of Fort Lauderdale.

What We Offer

Comprehensive Dental Services

From routine cleanings to advanced cosmetic and surgical procedures, our team provides complete dental care for the whole family.

Cleanings & Prevention

Regular exams, cleanings, and preventive care to keep your smile healthy for years to come.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Veneers, bonding, and smile makeovers designed to enhance your natural beauty.

Gum & Bone Treatment

Periodontal care, bone grafting, and treatments to maintain a strong foundation for your teeth.

Oral Surgery

Extractions, wisdom teeth removal, and surgical procedures performed with precision and care.

Orthodontics

Braces and alignment solutions for children and adults to achieve a straighter, healthier smile.

Pedodontics

Gentle, child-friendly dental care in a warm and welcoming environment.

Root Canal Therapy

Advanced endodontic treatment to save damaged teeth and relieve pain effectively.

Sedation Dentistry

Comfortable, anxiety-free dental visits with safe sedation options for nervous patients.

TMJ Treatment

Diagnosis and relief for jaw pain, headaches, and temporomandibular joint disorders.

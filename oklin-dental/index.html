Services

Everything your smile needs, under one roof.

General dentistry

A full range of general dental services, from routine cleanings and fillings to implant restorations.

Single-visit restorations

With CEREC technology your tooth is mapped in 3D and milled from a solid ceramic block, then placed in the same visit. No temporary cap, no second appointment.

ClearCorrect clear aligners

Straighten your teeth with clear aligner trays instead of traditional braces.

Oral surgery

Extractions of damaged teeth and wisdom teeth, handled with care so the experience stays simple.

Teeth whitening

Opalescence whitening, FDA-approved and used without light, heat or lasers. Choose in-office or take-home trays.

Cosmetic dentistry

Veneers, implants, whitening and more, planned around the smile you want.

Dental implant restoration

A titanium post replaces the root. Once it bonds to the bone, typically 3 to 6 months, a custom crown completes the tooth.

Snoring and sleep apnea

EMA, a noninvasive oral appliance for snoring and obstructive sleep apnea.

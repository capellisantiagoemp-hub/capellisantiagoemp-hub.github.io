About Us
Two Doctors, One Mission

At R&R Dental, Dr. Robert Singer and Dra. Rocío Montoya take pride in creating healthy and beautiful smiles. Centrally located in Kendall, across from Kendall Indian Hammocks Park, the practice welcomes patients of all ages.

Equipped with advanced dental technology — CEREC for same-day crowns, CBCT 3D imaging, and digital treatment planning — R&R Dental combines precision with a warm, personalized approach.

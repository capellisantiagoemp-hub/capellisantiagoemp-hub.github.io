Our Team

Meet your dentists.

Dr. Ludmila Tesoro

DDS

Dr. Ludmila Tesoro is a highly skilled dentist with a passion for providing full mouth restorations. Her education includes a General Practice Residency from the University of Miami/Jackson Memorial Hospital, where she completed full-time rotations in surgery, endodontics, periodontics, and prosthodontics.

She also holds a Certificate in Implantology from the Universidad Nacional Autónoma de México (1st place), an MSD with Specialization in Prosthodontics (1st Place with honors) and a Doctor in Dental Surgery degree from the Universidad Central de Venezuela.

Ludmila is a member of the American Dental Association, Florida Dental Association, and South Florida District Dental Association since November 2021. She is also the President of the Latin American Academy of Osseo integrated Dental Implants since June 2023.

Dr. Justin Schneider

Oral Maxillofacial Surgeon

Dr. Justin Schneider is an accomplished Oral Maxillofacial Surgeon with extensive experience in private practice and multiple hospital systems. He specializes in a wide range of services, providing exceptional care to his patients, from dental implants to wisdom teeth extractions under IV sedation, bone grafting, sinus lifts, and augmentation.
